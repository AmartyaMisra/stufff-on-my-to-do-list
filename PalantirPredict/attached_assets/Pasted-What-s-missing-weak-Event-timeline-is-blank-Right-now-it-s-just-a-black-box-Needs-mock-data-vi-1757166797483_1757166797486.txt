What’s missing / weak

Event timeline is blank
Right now it’s just a black box. Needs mock data + visualization (bars, dots, heatmap). Even if fake, must “move.”

Entity panel is barren
“No entity selected” is too empty. You want a placeholder card with risk gauge, dummy attributes, and expandable evidence.
→ Otherwise users feel like nothing works.

No visual graph
Analysts expect at least a node-link ego view. Even a small cytoscape.js canvas showing one node connected to “dummy IP / dummy txn” would instantly elevate.

Evidence chain missing
Right now it says “explainable evidence chains” in the description, but UI doesn’t show SHAP values, keywords, or “top signals.” That’s the magic sauce.

Aesthetic polish
Buttons are okay, but inconsistent: CSV = light blue, Pause = yellow, Filters = dark grey. Needs unified palette (e.g., neon cyan primary, amber secondary, red for critical).

Populate timeline with synthetic mock alerts every 5 seconds:

Risk gauge (progress circle 0–100).

Top evidence tokens highlighted in red (“detonate”, “package”).

Step 3 — Semi-AI scoring (lightweight, free):

Backend:

sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 for embeddings.

scikit-learn LogisticRegression on toy labeled set (violent / neutral).
